Clinical plasma concentration-time profiles were taken from the published studies compiled for the model publication [Marok 2023](References.md#5-references). The dataset covers oral single-dose and multiple-dose ketoconazole administration in adults, fasted and fed states, and doses from 100 mg to 1200 mg.

| Publication | Data used |
| --- | --- |
| [Heel 1982](References.md#5-references) | Oral solution and tablet ketoconazole studies in adults under fasted conditions. |
| [Daneshmend 1981](References.md#5-references), [Daneshmend 1983](References.md#5-references), [Daneshmend 1984](References.md#5-references) | Oral ketoconazole studies in adults covering fasted, fed, single-dose and multiple-dose administration. |
| [Huang 1986](References.md#5-references) | Oral solution and tablet ketoconazole data in healthy adults. |
| [Polk 1999](References.md#5-references), [Boyce 2012](References.md#5-references), [Tiseo 1998](References.md#5-references), [Craven 1983](References.md#5-references), [Männistö 1982](References.md#5-references), [Greenblatt 1998](References.md#5-references) | Oral ketoconazole clinical data used to evaluate formulation, food-effect and multiple-dose performance. |
| [Weiss 2022](References.md#5-references) | Oral ketoconazole study in adults with plasma profiles for ketoconazole and N-deacetylketoconazole. |

Physicochemical, transporter and metabolic information was taken from experimental literature and in silico calculations. Ketoconazole and metabolite pK<sub>a</sub>, lipophilicity and solubility parameters were informed by Chemicalize and formulation-relevant dissolution data, while in vitro metabolism and inhibition data were taken from studies on AADAC, CYP3A4, P-gp and UGT pathways [Fukami 2016](References.md#5-references), [Fitch 2009](References.md#5-references), [Schwab 2003](References.md#5-references), [Bourcier 2010](References.md#5-references), [Weiss 2022](References.md#5-references).

The FMO3 K<sub>m</sub> value of 1.17 µmol/L implemented for N-deacetylketoconazole is correct. The corresponding value reported by [Marok 2023](References.md#5-references) is erroneous.
